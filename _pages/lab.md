The **Emotion & Technology (E.T) Lab** is directed by [Jonas P. Schöne](/) and is based in the [School of Psychology](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/about/faculties-schools-departments/school-of-psychology) at the University of Southampton. We investigate how emotional expressions are shaped, perceived, and experienced in technology-mediated environments. We combine experimental methods, computational tools, and large-scale data analysis to understand the interplay between human emotion and digital platforms.

## Research Partnerships

The E.T Lab maintains close research collaborations with leading groups at Harvard and Stanford:

- **[Goldenberg Lab](https://www.amitgoldenberg.com/)** (Harvard Business School) -- Joint work on emotion perception, amplification, and collective emotion dynamics. PI: [Amit Goldenberg](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/profile.aspx?facId=1189871).
- **[Politics and Social Change Lab](https://www.pascl.stanford.edu/)** (Stanford University) -- Joint work on digital well-being, AI interventions, and social media. PI: [Robb Willer](https://sociology.stanford.edu/people/robb-willer).
- **[Stanford Culture and Emotion Lab](https://culture-emotion-lab.stanford.edu/)** (Stanford University) -- Joint work on content filtering and affective well-being. PI: [Jeanne Tsai](https://profiles.stanford.edu/jeanne-tsai).
- **[Stanford Computational Psychology and Well-Being Lab](https://profiles.stanford.edu/eich)** (Stanford University) -- Joint work on AI chatbot interventions and social media language for well-being. PI: [Johannes Eichstaedt](https://profiles.stanford.edu/eich).

## Research Directions

### AI and Social Media

How do social media platforms amplify, distort, or filter the emotional content people encounter? We study how algorithmic feeds shape exposure to negativity, how users perceive the emotional tone of online discourse, and how AI-based interventions can improve digital well-being. This work includes building browser extensions that modify real social media feeds, designing AI chatbots for psychological interventions, and analyzing billions of social media posts to measure population well-being.

### Sports, Organisational Behaviour, and Collective Performance

How do emotions spread within teams and organisations, and how does this affect collective performance? We develop and validate psychological assessment tools for elite sport, study how emotional contagion operates in group settings, and examine how people form moral judgments about organisations based on the emotional expressions of their members. This line of work bridges basic research on emotion perception with applied questions in sports psychology and organisational behaviour.

### Emotion Sharing and Perceiving

How do people integrate multiple emotional expressions into an overall impression? We study the cognitive mechanisms behind emotion perception -- including the tendency to overestimate emotional intensity when aggregating across expressions -- and how these biases operate in both face-to-face and digital contexts. This foundational work informs our understanding of why online environments can feel more emotionally intense than they really are.

## Join the Lab

We are actively recruiting graduate students to join the E.T Lab starting in the **2027--2028 academic year**. I am looking for motivated students across all three research directions above.

### Funded and Self-Funded Opportunities

I especially encourage applications from students who are able to secure external funding, and I am happy to support you through the application process. Several competitive scholarships are available for international students, including:

- **[Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes](https://www.studienstiftung.de/)** -- Germany's most prestigious academic scholarship, available to German nationals pursuing doctoral study abroad
- **[China Scholarship Council (CSC)](https://www.chinesescholarshipcouncil.com/)** -- Full funding for Chinese nationals pursuing PhD programmes in the UK
- **Commonwealth Scholarships**, **Marshall Scholarships**, and other national funding bodies

If you hold or are eligible for a national scholarship from your home country, I would be glad to discuss how we can work together to build a strong application.

Please email me at [jonaspaul.schoene@gmail.com](mailto:jonaspaul.schoene@gmail.com) with:
