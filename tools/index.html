Well-Being Chatbots

These chatbots were developed as part of the AI and Emotional Well-Being initiative at the Stanford AI for Public Benefit Lab (with Robb Willer and Johannes Eichstaedt). Each chatbot guides users through a brief, structured conversation grounded in well-being research. In a preregistered experiment (N = 2,922), these dialogues produced short-term improvements on several well-being outcomes. See Schöne et al. (under review) for details.

Social Media Well-Being Filter

A browser-based tool that lets users filter negative content from their social media feeds in real time. Building on our research showing that exposure to algorithmically amplified negativity harms well-being, this tool will give people control over what emotional content they encounter online. Details and a public release to follow.
