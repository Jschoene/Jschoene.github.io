Industry

Industry partnerships and applied research.

Industry Partnerships

My research has always had a strong applied dimension. I believe that the best science emerges when academic rigour meets real-world problems, and I actively seek partnerships with organisations where insights from emotion and behavioural science can make a tangible difference.

Current Partnerships

Game1.ai (2026 – present) – Scientific Advisor

I work with Game1.ai to conceptualise, operationalise, and validate standardised psychological test batteries that evaluate cognitive, emotional, and behavioural metrics in academy soccer players. This collaboration bridges my research on emotion perception and measurement with the practical demands of talent identification in elite sport.

Previous Applied Work

Harvard Business School – D3 Institute (2022 – 2023)

As a Visiting Graduate Research Fellow at Harvard’s Digital, Data, and Design Institute, I translated behavioural research into digital transformation strategies for Fortune 500 companies and presented recommendations on platform design and AI governance to MBAs, C-suite executives, and entrepreneurs.

Leicester City Football Club Academy (2017 – 2018)

I designed psychological resilience assessments for Premier League youth player selection that were adopted into the club’s official recruitment process. I also introduced evidence-based decision-making tools based on psychometrics to coaching and senior management.

Norwegian Cyber Defence Force (2016 – 2017)

I developed and validated stress resilience and decision-making assessments for use in live operational conditions, contributing to improved officer cadet selection criteria.

Collaborate With Me

I am keen to develop new partnerships in two areas:

Sports organisations – I bring expertise in psychological assessment, emotion measurement, and behavioural analytics. Whether you are looking to improve talent identification, support athlete well-being, or build evidence-based decision-making tools, I would welcome a conversation.

AI and technology companies – My research on digital well-being, content filtering, and AI-driven psychological interventions has direct implications for product design, platform governance, and responsible AI. I am particularly interested in collaborations that explore how AI systems can be designed to support rather than undermine emotional well-being.
